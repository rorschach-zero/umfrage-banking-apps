Data protection

What happens to your answers

This page belongs to the survey "Survey on banking apps". It says in plain words who collects the data, what data that is, where it is stored, who sees it and when it is deleted.

Who is responsible

A group of five students of the College of Higher Education in Business (HFW) at KV Business School Zürich, class 601/H25-B, as part of a practical project. Responsible person for the student group: Salah Aouaj; contact for questions, access or deletion: umfrage@hydeout.ch.

What the data is used for

For a practical project in collaboration with a Swiss banking app. It is the project partner, it did not commission the data collection, and it is named at the end of the survey so that answers remain unbiased. The project examines what customers expect from the customer service of a banking app and how they experience it.

What data is collected

  • Your answers to the survey questions, including age group, canton, banking apps used and ratings.
  • An identifier. It is generated at random when the page loads or comes from the link you used to open the survey. Our links only contain an abbreviation for the distribution channel, for example "SCH-" for the school; the page adds random characters. We do not issue links that identify a person. The identifier serves to detect duplicate submissions and to analyse the distribution channel. You see it at the end of the survey, also if you are screened out.
  • The survey version, the time from loading to sending in seconds, whether you were on a phone or a computer, the chosen language, the time your answers arrived and, if your browser sends it, the domain name of the page from which you opened the link (for example reddit.com; usually empty with WhatsApp). We use it to count through which channels the survey was shared.
  • If you are not part of the target group (place of residence, age, app use), the survey ends at the first of these up to three questions that rules you out. Only the answers given up to that point, the identifier, the version, the chosen language, the question at which you were screened out, phone or computer and the time of receipt are then stored, without you needing to press a send button. This is stated before the start as well.
  • If you stop before sending, we store no answers. We only record the question at which you stopped, together with the identifier, the version, the language, phone or computer, the referring site and the time elapsed up to that point. This shows us where the survey is too long or unclear. Your identifier is shown at the bottom of every page, even if you stop early.
  • If you provide it voluntarily: your e-mail address, only for the Galaxus voucher prize draw and to send you the results (prize draw rules).

The survey itself does not collect your name, account details, IP address or location.

Who sees the data

Only the five students of the group see the raw data. The HFW lecturers and the project partner receive analysed results, that is, percentages, averages and summarised free-text answers, not individual answer rows.

The limits of anonymity, honestly

The survey does not ask for your name. Still: anyone who writes something personal in a free-text field, for example a name or a rare incident, can become recognisable. So do not write anything that identifies you or others. The combination of age group, canton and apps used can also apply to only a few people in small groups. That is why results are only reported for groups with enough answers, and free texts are cleaned of personal details before being passed on.

How long the data is kept

E-mail addresses are deleted as soon as the voucher has been delivered (after a redraw if there is no reply) and the results have been sent, at the latest after the grades are published. The answers are deleted after the grades for the practical project are published, and completely: the table at Google, all local copies and exports on the group's computers. The analysed results (percentages, averages, cleaned summaries) remain part of the written project. The progress data (point where you stopped) is deleted together with the answers.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time; without "Send answers" your answers are not stored, with the exception mentioned above in case of exclusion after the first three questions. The point at which you stopped is recorded without answers, as described above. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. For this you need the identifier shown to you at the end, also if you are screened out. Send it to the contact address. Without the identifier we cannot find your row, because we do not know which row is yours.

Legal basis

The collection follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the principles of market research (ICC/ESOMAR code): voluntariness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation.
