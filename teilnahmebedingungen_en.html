Prize draw rules

Prize draw for a Galaxus voucher

Among everyone who completes the survey "Survey on banking apps" and leaves an e-mail address at the end, we will draw one winner of a Galaxus gift voucher worth CHF 100.

1. Organiser

A group of five students of the HFW at KV Business School Zürich, class 601/H25-B, as part of a practical project. Responsible person for the student group: Salah Aouaj; contact: umfrage@hydeout.ch. The project partner (a Swiss banking app, named at the end of the survey) and the school are not involved in the prize draw.

2. Participation

  • Participation is free and not tied to any purchase.
  • You can take part if you are aged 16 to 65, live in Switzerland and use a banking app on your phone (the survey's target group).
  • To take part: complete the survey up to "Send answers" and leave an e-mail address on the final screen.
  • One entry per person. Multiple entries, false information and technical manipulation lead to exclusion.
  • The five members of the student group and people in their households are excluded.
  • Closing date: 1 December 2026, 11.59 pm.

3. Prize

One Galaxus gift voucher worth CHF 100, delivered by e-mail. No cash alternative, no exchange.

4. Prize draw and notification

The draw takes place on 8 December 2026 at random among all valid e-mail addresses, in private; it is documented in the practical project without naming addresses. The winner is notified by e-mail on the same day. If the winner does not respond within 14 days, a new draw is made. No correspondence is entered into regarding the prize draw, except for notifying the winner and for data protection requests.

5. Privacy

The e-mail address is stored separately from the answers, only with the month, and used solely for the draw and for sending the results. No advertising, no disclosure to third parties. It is deleted once the prize has been delivered, any redraw has taken place and the results have been sent, at the latest after the grades are published. Everything else is on the page Privacy notice for the survey.

6. Final provisions

Swiss law applies. The organiser may adapt or cancel the raffle in case of technical faults or abuse; participants are informed on this page. This is a translation of the German text, which prevails. Last updated: 30 October 2026.
